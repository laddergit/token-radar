What this radar does not do

  • It cannot see points programs, Discord roles or private fundraisings — the farm plan tells you how to check for them per protocol.
  • TVL multiples undervalue volume businesses (prediction markets, perps venues) whose money is made on flow, not on locked capital. The category median partly corrects for this, but treat the implied value as an order-of-magnitude estimate, not a price target.
  • A protocol can stay tokenless forever. Farming is a bet on a launch that may never happen — never deposit more than you can lose.

Data: DefiLlama public protocols API, sealed snapshot . Analysis runs locally in main.ts — no live prices, no tracking, no network calls from this page.

Not financial advice. Implied values are model estimates from TVL multiples, not valuations. Always verify a protocol's official site from DefiLlama before connecting a wallet.
